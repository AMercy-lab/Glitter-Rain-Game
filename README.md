# Glitter Rain 💖

A girly catch-'em game for [start.ng](https://start.ng). Your own start.ng character holds a pink basket and catches falling hearts, bows, lipsticks, cupcakes and diamonds. Dodge the storm clouds and broken hearts!

- **Move:** ← → / A D, mouse, or drag your finger
- **Points:** 💖 10 · 🎀 15 · 🧁 15 · 💄 20 · 💎 50. Catch them in a row to build a combo (up to x5)
- **Power-ups:** ⭐ sparkle shield · 🧲 magnet · 💝 extra life
- **Pause:** P or Esc

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so there's nothing to build. Open `index.html` to play. Sign-in and the leaderboard work once it's live on start.ng.
